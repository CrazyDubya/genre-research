## How to Use These Breakdowns

1. **Choose a film** similar to your project's tone/subgenre
2. **Study the structure** - note how fear builds act by act
3. **Identify the scare beats** - when and why they occur
4. **Analyze character arcs** - how characters change through fear
5. **Note the rules** - what constraints govern the horror?
6. **Adapt, don't copy** - understand principles, not just plot points

## Horror Structure Principles

### Three-Act Horror Framework
- **Act 1**: Establish normal, introduce threat (subtle)
- **Act 2**: Escalate threat, protagonist struggles, fails
- **Act 3**: Confrontation, climax, resolution (or lack thereof)

### Fear Escalation Pattern
1. **Unease** (something's off)
2. **Dread** (something bad will happen)
3. **Terror** (something bad is happening)
4. **Horror** (seeing/experiencing the bad thing)

### Common Structural Elements
- **The Rules**: Every horror has constraints (sunlight kills vampires, silence keeps monsters away)
- **The Reveal**: When/how the audience learns the threat
- **False Security**: Moments of relief before next scare
- **The Cost**: What characters sacrifice to survive
- **The Final Image**: What lingers after the story ends
