## Example 7: Night of the Living Dead (1968)
**Type:** Zombie Apocalypse + Siege Survival
**Structure:** Escalating Threat + Siege
**Directed by George A. Romero

### Threat Profile

**Threat:** Reanimated corpses (zombies/ghouls)

**Threat's Nature:**
- Recently dead returning to life
- Driven to eat living flesh
- Can be killed by destroying brain
- Spreading (bites turn victims)

**Threat's Rules:**
1. Recently dead return to life (cause unknown)
2. Eat living flesh (driven by hunger)
3. Slow but relentless
4. Destroy brain to kill (headshots, blunt trauma)
5. Bites not shown to spread (infection not established in first film)

**Threat's Origin:**
- Radiation from space probe (suggested, never confirmed)
- "They're coming to get you, Barbra!"

**Threat's Weakness:**
- Can be killed (destroy brain)
- Slow (can be outrun)
- Can be boarded out (barricades work temporarily)

### Character Arc

**Ben - The Leader/Survivor**

**Start:**
- Arrives at farmhouse (rescues Barbra)
- Takes charge (boards up house)
- Resourceful, competent
- Tensions with Harry (conflict over leadership)

**Midpoint:**
- Defends house from zombie attacks
- Tries to maintain order
- Radio reports: widespread outbreak

**End:**
- Shoots Harry (conflict over gun)
- Survives night
- Morning: posse arrives
- **Ben killed** (mistaken for zombie, shot in head)

**Harry Cooper - The Skeptic/Conflict**

**Start:**
- Wife Helen, daughter Karen (sick from bite)
- Wants to hide in cellar (safer)
- Conflicts with Ben (wants upstairs)

**Midpoint:**
- Tensions escalate (leadership disputes)
- Karen dies, reanimates
- Harry shot by Ben

**End:**
- Dies (shot by Ben)
- Body reanimates (eaten by zombies—ironic)

**Barbra - The First Victim**

**Start:**
- Visiting grave with brother Johnny
- Zombie attacks, Johnny killed
- Flees to farmhouse (traumatized)

**Midpoint:**
- Catatonic (in shock)
- Ben tries to help

**End:**
- Pulled into zombie horde (eaten)
- First victim, sets stakes

### Structure Breakdown

**Act One (Setup - 30 minutes):**
- Cemetery: Barbra and Johnny, zombie attacks, Johnny killed
- Barbra flees to farmhouse (encounters Ben)
- Ben boards up house (siege preparation)
- Harry, Helen, Karen in cellar (reveal themselves)
- Tensions: upstairs vs. cellar, Ben vs. Harry

**Act Two (Confrontation - 45 minutes):**
- First zombie attack: boards pried off, zombie killed
- Radio reports: widespread outbreaks, "safe zones"
- Karen dies (sick from bite)
- Zombie attacks increase (more numerous, bolder)
- Harry shot by Ben (conflict over gun)
- Karen reanimates, eats Helen
- House besieged (zombies breaking in)
- Ben trapped in cellar (Helen eats Harry)

**Act Three (Resolution - 15 minutes):**
- Ben survives night
- Morning: posse arrives (with dogs)
- **Ben shot** (mistaken for zombie)
- Zombies killed (bodies burned)
- News montage: posse successful, but tragic ending

### Why It Works

1. **The Zombie Template:** Established modern zombie mythos
2. **Siege Structure:** Trapped in farmhouse (claustrophobic)
3. **Escalating Threat:** Zombies increase (more numerous, more aggressive)
4. **Social Commentary:** 1968, Civil Rights era (Ben's death tragic irony)
5. **Low Budget but Effective:** Simple story, black and white, limited locations
6. **Tragic Ending:** Hero doesn't survive (subverts expectations)

### Key Scare Scenes

1. **Cemetery attack:** "They're coming to get you, Barbra!" (first zombie)
2. **First farmhouse zombie:** Ben kills it with tire iron (demonstrates how to kill)
3. **Karen reanimates:** Eats mother Helen (family tragedy horror)
4. **Cellar scene:** Helen eating Harry (Ben trapped, discovers horror)
5. **Ben's death:** Shot by posse (mistaken for zombie—tragic irony)

### Rule Analysis

**Rule 1:** Recently dead return to life
- **Why:** Creates escalating threat (every death becomes new enemy)

**Rule 2:** Destroy brain to kill
- **Why:** Established weakness (enables survival)

**Rule 3:** Slow but relentless
- **Why:** Siege horror (can be escaped but keeps coming)
