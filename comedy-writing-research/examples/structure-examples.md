## How to Use These Examples

1. **Start with the format** closest to your project (sitcom, film, sketch, etc.)
2. **Study the structure** - note how beats are timed and paced
3. **Identify the game** - what's the core comic mechanism?
4. **Adapt, don't copy** - use the structure as a framework, not a template
5. **Mix and match** - combine elements from different structures

## Key Principles Across All Formats

- **Rule of Three**: Patterns repeat three times (setup, callback, bigger callback)
- **Escalation**: Each beat should raise the stakes
- **Callbacks**: Early jokes pay off later
- **Subversion**: Set expectations, then flip them
- **Character truth**: Comedy comes from who people are, not just what happens
